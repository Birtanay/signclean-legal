2. Information Stored Locally On Your Device

The app stores basic non-personal gameplay data locally on your device using standard Apple storage (UserDefaults). This includes:

  • In-game scores, streak counts, and unlocked signs or tools.
  • Sound, haptic feedback, and local notification preferences.
  • The local counter for total signs cleaned.

This information stays entirely on your device, is never transmitted to our servers, and is automatically removed when you uninstall or delete the app.

3. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs

To keep the app free to play, SignClean ASMR displays third-party advertisements provided by Google AdMob. While we do not operate external servers, third-party advertising SDKs integrated into the app collect and process certain technical identifiers automatically.

Categories of Data Processed by Ad Partners

  • Unique Device Identifiers: Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and IP address.
  • General Technical Data: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Coarse Location Data: General geographic location (country or city level) derived from your IP address to show localized ads.
  • Usage & Diagnostic Data: Interactions with advertisements, crash reports, and app performance logs.

Managing Ad Tracking & Personalization

  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS 14.5 or later, the app presents an ATT prompt asking for permission to track your activity across apps for targeted advertising.
  • Consent Management (UK/EEA): For users in the UK or European Economic Area (EEA), we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent banner upon initial launch to request consent for data processing and personalized ads.
  • Declining/Revoking Tracking: If you decline permission or opt out, you will still see advertisements, but they will be non-personalized (based on general context). You can change your choices at any time in your iOS device settings via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Learn more about how Google uses data by visiting Google's Partner Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (UK / EU GDPR)

Under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, we rely on the following legal grounds to process device and advertising data:

  • Consent: When you grant permission via Apple's ATT prompt or Google's UMP banner to allow personalized advertising and tracking identifiers. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate Interests: To display contextual (non-personalized) ads, secure the app against fraud or abuse, ensure network stability, and monitor general technical performance.

5. Your Privacy Rights

A. European Union & United Kingdom Rights (UK/EU GDPR)

If you reside in the EEA or UK, you have the following rights regarding personal data collected by third-party SDKs:

  • Right to Access / Portability: Request information regarding what data third-party ad networks hold about your device identifier.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Request deletion of data linked to your Advertising Identifier.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke previously granted ad tracking consent via your iOS device settings.
  • Right to Object / Restrict Processing: Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise these rights regarding ad data, reset or restrict your iOS Advertising Identifier or email us at ay.birtan@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (such as the UK Information Commissioner's Office — ICO).

B. California & US State Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and state privacy laws:

  • Right to Know and Delete: California residents can request disclosures regarding the categories of personal information collected, or request deletion of data associated with their device.
  • Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: We do not "sell" personal information in the traditional monetary sense. However, utilizing third-party advertising SDKs that collect IDFA or technical identifiers for cross-context behavioral advertising may be considered "sharing" or a "sale" under CCPA.
  • Opting Out: You can opt out of targeted ad processing at any time by selecting "Ask App Not to Track" during the ATT prompt, turning off tracking in your iOS device settings, or setting your preference in the in-app consent menu.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

6. What We Do Not Collect

We do not require account creation, and we do not collect or request:

  • Real names, postal addresses, phone numbers, or email addresses (except when you voluntarily contact us for support).
  • Precise GPS location data.

If you send us a support email, we will only use your email address to reply to your inquiry.

You can erase this data by resetting your Advertising Identifier via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iOS device or by contacting us.
